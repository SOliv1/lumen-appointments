🌟 What Lumen Means
Lumen is a Latin‑derived word meaning light or illumination.
In modern usage, it has three common domains:

Science / Physics  
A lumen is a unit of light output. Neutral. Technical. Widely recognised.

Healthcare / Medical Terminology  
A lumen is the inside space of a tube or vessel (e.g., in anatomy).
This makes the word familiar in clinical environments without being branded.

Technology / Software Naming  
Many modern tools use “Lumen” to signal clarity, intelligence, or clean design.

Because of these three domains, Lumen feels professional, modern, and safe — and importantly, it does not belong to the crowded “care / sync / serene / wellbeing” naming cluster.
