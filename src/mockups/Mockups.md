## GMC Numbers (Doctors)

- `7654321`

## NMC PINs (Nurses & Midwives)
Two letters + six digits.

- `AB123456`
- `NM482917`
- `CS309184`
- `RN558291`
- `HV764321`

## HCPC Registration Numbers (Allied Health)
Two letters + five digits.

- `OT12345` (Occupational Therapist)
- `PT48291` (Physiotherapist)
- `PR30918` (Paramedic)
- `RA55829` (Radiographer)
- `SL76432` (Speech & Language Therapist)
